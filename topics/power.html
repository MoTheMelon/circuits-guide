Power sources & regulators

Most projects run from a battery or adapter at one voltage and need a clean, lower voltage for logic. A linear regulator (7805, AMS1117, LDOs) burns the difference as heat — simple and quiet, but wasteful at large drops. A switching (buck) converter chops and filters, reaching ~85–95% efficiency.

  • Input/output capsEvery regulator needs the capacitors its datasheet specifies; some LDOs oscillate without them.
  • DropoutA 7805 needs ~2 V of headroom; an LDO 0.1–0.5 V.
  • Fuse / polyfuseIn series with the supply to stop a short from becoming a fire.
  • Reverse-polarity protectionSeries Schottky diode (simple, ~0.3 V loss) or a P-MOSFET (near-zero loss).
