Rectifiers & smoothing

Rectifiers turn AC into pulsating DC using diodes; a big capacitor then fills in the gaps. A half-wave rectifier uses one diode and throws away half the waveform. A full-wave bridge uses four diodes so both halves charge the capacitor, halving ripple at the cost of two diode drops.

Vripple ≈ Iloadfr · C
fr = mains f (half-wave) or 2× mains f (full-wave).
Vpeak,out = Vp − n·VF
n = 1 diode (half-wave) or 2 (bridge).
Vp = √2 · Vrms
A "12 V AC" transformer gives ~17 V peak.
