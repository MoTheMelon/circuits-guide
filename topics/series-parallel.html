Series & parallel: total resistance

In series, parts share one path, so the same current flows through all of them and resistances add. In parallel, parts share the same two nodes, so they see the same voltage and their conductances (1/R) add.

Req = R1 + R2 + …

1Req = 1R1 + 1R2 + …
Parallel resistors.
R1∥R2 = R1R2R1+R2
"Product over sum" — two in parallel. Two equal resistors give R/2.
Cpar = C1 + C2
Capacitors combine the opposite way: add in parallel, reciprocal in series. Inductors combine like resistors.
