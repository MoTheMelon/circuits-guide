Transformer

Two coils sharing a magnetic core. An AC current in the primary creates a changing flux that induces a voltage in the secondary, scaled by the turns ratio. Transformers step voltage up or down, isolate circuits from mains, and match impedances. They do nothing at DC.

VsVp = NsNp
Voltage follows the turns ratio.
IsIp = NpNs
Current goes the other way, so power in ≈ power out.
Zp = (Np/Ns)² ZL
Load impedance reflected to the primary.
Safety.

Anything connected to mains (120/230 V AC) can kill. Use a pre-built, certified wall adapter for projects rather than wiring transformers to mains yourself.
