Thévenin & Norton equivalents

Any linear network seen from two terminals behaves exactly like a single voltage source Vth in series with a single resistance Rth (Thévenin), or a current source IN in parallel with RN = Rth (Norton). It is how you reason about "output impedance" and loading.

Vth = Vopen-circuit
Measure or compute the voltage with nothing connected.
Rth = Voc / Isc
Or: zero all sources (short V sources, open I sources) and find R between the terminals.
IN = Vth / Rth
Norton current = short-circuit current.
Pmax = Vth²4Rth
Maximum power transfer occurs when RL = Rth (efficiency is only 50% there).
