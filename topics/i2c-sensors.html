I²C sensors & pull-ups

Many sensors — IMUs (accelerometer + gyroscope), temperature/humidity, light, ToF distance, OLEDs — talk over I²C: two wires, SDA (data) and SCL (clock), shared by many devices each with an address. Both lines are open-drain: devices only pull them low, so resistors must pull them high.

Devices must sink at least 3 mA.
  • WiringVCC, GND, SDA, SCL. Keep wires short (bus capacitance grows ~50–100 pF per metre).
  • Voltage levelPull up to the lower of the two logic voltages. Mixing 5 V and 3.3 V devices needs a level shifter.
  • Address conflictsTwo identical sensors often need an address-select pin tied differently, or an I²C multiplexer.
  • DebugRun an I²C scan first. No devices found usually means swapped SDA/SCL, missing pull-ups or no power.
