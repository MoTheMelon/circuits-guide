Potentiometer

A pot is a resistor with a sliding third terminal (the wiper). Wired as a voltage divider (ends to Vcc and GND, wiper to an ADC) it turns knob position into a voltage — the standard way to read a knob or joystick axis. Wired with just one end and the wiper, it is a variable resistor (rheostat).

VW = Vin · RWBRtotal
Unloaded wiper voltage.

Linear ("B") taper is right for sensing position. Audio ("A", logarithmic) taper matches how we hear loudness. 10 kΩ is a good default for microcontroller inputs — low enough to feed an ADC, high enough to waste only 0.33 mA at 3.3 V.
