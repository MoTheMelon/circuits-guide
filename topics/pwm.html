Pulse-width modulation (PWM)

PWM fakes an analog level by switching fully on and fully off very fast. The load (a motor's inductance, an LED viewed by your eye, an RC filter) averages the pulses. Because the switch is always fully on or fully off, it wastes almost no power — that's why every motor driver and LED dimmer uses it.
