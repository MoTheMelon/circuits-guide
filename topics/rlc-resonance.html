RLC resonance

With an inductor and a capacitor together, their reactances cancel at one frequency, the resonant frequency. A series RLC circuit draws maximum current there; the resistance alone sets how tall and narrow the peak is. Resonance underlies radio tuning, oscillators, wireless charging and unwanted ringing on PCB traces.
