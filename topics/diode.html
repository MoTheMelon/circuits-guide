Diode

A diode lets current flow one way — from anode to cathode (the bar on the symbol, the stripe on the part) — once the forward voltage is exceeded, and blocks it in reverse until breakdown. Uses: reverse-polarity protection, rectification, flyback clamping, signal steering and OR-ing power sources.

I = IS(eV/(n VT) − 1)
Shockley diode equation. VT = kT/q ≈ 25.9 mV at room temperature; n ≈ 1–2.
VF ≈ 0.6–0.7 V
Silicon. Schottky ≈ 0.2–0.4 V (fast, leakier). LEDs 1.8–3.3 V.
+60 mV per 10× I
Exponential: forward voltage creeps up only slightly as current rises (for n = 1). Vf drops ~2 mV/°C.
