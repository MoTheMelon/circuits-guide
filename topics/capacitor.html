Capacitor & RC charging

A capacitor stores energy in an electric field between two plates. It opposes changes in voltage: current flows only while the voltage is changing. That makes caps the go-to part for smoothing supplies, decoupling ICs, timing, filtering and blocking DC.

Q = C · V
Charge stored (coulombs). C in farads; real parts are pF to mF.
i = C dvdt
Current is proportional to how fast voltage changes. Voltage can't jump instantly.
τ = R · C
Time constant: 63% charged after 1τ, ~99% after 5τ.
v(t) = Vs(1 − e−t/τ)
Charging. Discharging: V0e−t/τ.
E = ½ C V²
Energy stored (joules).
XC = 12πf C
Reactance: open circuit at DC, near-short at high frequency.

What a real circuit needs

  • Decoupling100 nF ceramic as close as possible to every IC power pin, plus 1–10 µF bulk per board section.
  • PolarityElectrolytic and tantalum caps are polarized: stripe = negative. Reversed, they can vent or burn.
  • Voltage ratingPick ≥ 1.5–2× the working voltage. Ceramic (X5R/X7R) caps lose much of their capacitance at high DC bias.
  • Codes"104" = 10 × 10⁴ pF = 100 nF. "105" = 1 µF. "220" = 22 pF.
