Thermistor (NTC)

An NTC thermistor's resistance drops as it warms. It is cheap and sensitive, but non-linear, so firmware converts the ADC reading back to temperature with the Beta (or Steinhart–Hart) equation.

R(T) = R0 eB(1/T − 1/T0)
Beta model. T in kelvin; T0 = 298.15 K (25 °C).
1T = 1T0 + 1B lnRR0
Inverse, used in firmware.

Choose the fixed resistor equal to the thermistor's resistance at the middle of the range you care about. Keep the divider current low (tens of µA) so the thermistor doesn't heat itself.
