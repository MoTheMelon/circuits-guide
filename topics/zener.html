Zener diode regulator

A Zener is built to break down at a precise reverse voltage and hold it. Placed in reverse across a load with a series resistor, it makes a simple shunt regulator or a voltage reference, and it is commonly used to clamp inputs against overvoltage.

Rs = Vin(min) − VZIL(max) + IZ(min)
Design Rs for the worst case: lowest input, highest load. Iz(min) ≈ 1–5 mA.
PZ = VZ · IZ
Worst case is highest input with no load. Common parts are 0.5 W or 1 W.
