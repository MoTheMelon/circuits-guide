Bipolar junction transistor (BJT)

A BJT is a current-controlled switch and amplifier: a small base current lets a much larger collector current flow. NPN (e.g. 2N2222, 2N3904, BC547) switches the low side of a load — between load and ground. PNP (2N3906, BC557) switches the high side. The arrow is on the emitter and points the way conventional current flows.
