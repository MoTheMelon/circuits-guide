Photoresistor (LDR)

A light-dependent resistor's resistance falls as light increases — from around 1 MΩ in darkness to a few hundred ohms in bright light. Like any resistive sensor, you read it with a voltage divider into an ADC.

R ≈ R10 (E/10 lx)−γ
Power-law response; γ ≈ 0.6–0.9.
Vout = Vcc·RbottomRtop+Rbottom
Divider — same formula for every resistive sensor.
