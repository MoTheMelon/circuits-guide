Voltage divider

Two resistors in series split a voltage in proportion to their values. It is the most-used sub-circuit in electronics: reading sensors, scaling a 5 V signal down to 3.3 V, setting bias points and regulator feedback.

Vout = Vin·R2R1+R2
Unloaded output (nothing draws current from Vout).
R2′ = R2 ∥ RL
With a load, replace R2 by R2 in parallel with the load.
Level shifting 5 V → 3.3 V.

R1 = 1.8 kΩ, R2 = 3.3 kΩ gives 3.24 V. Fine for slow signals into a high-impedance input; for fast or bidirectional lines (I²C) use a level-shifter.
