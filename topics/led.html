LED & current-limiting resistor

An LED is a diode that emits light. Because its current rises exponentially with voltage, you must never connect one straight across a supply; a series resistor sets the current. The long leg is the anode (+); the flat side of the case marks the cathode.

Driving from a GPIO.

A microcontroller pin can typically source or sink only a few to ~12 mA (check the datasheet; the RP2040's default drive is 4 mA). For more current or many LEDs, switch them with a transistor or use a constant-current LED driver.
